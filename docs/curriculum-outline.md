## 3. Programme Core — 47 AU (all compulsory)

### Year 1 Semester 1
- SC1004 Linear Algebra for Computing (4) — SC1003 co-req — vectors, matrices, eigen
- SC1005 Digital Logic (3) — Nil — Boolean algebra, combinational/sequential circuits
- MH1812 Discrete Mathematics / SC1123+SC1124 Math 1 & 2 for Computing (3) — cohort-dependent (CSC U24 = MH1812 per PDF)
> Note: SC1003 (3 AU, Nil) is Foundational Core, not Programme Core — counted in College Core §2 to avoid double-count. Programme Core 47 AU = SC1004(4)+SC1005(3)+MH1812(3)+SC1006(3)+SC1007(3)+SC1008(3)+SC2000(3)+SC2002(3)+SC2001(3)+SC2005(3)+SC2203(3)+SC2006(3)+SC2008(3)+SC2207(3)+SC3099(4).

### Year 1 Semester 2
- SC1006 Computer Organisation & Architecture (3) — SC1005 co-req — datapath, pipeline, cache
- SC1007 Data Structures & Algorithms (3) — SC1003 — arrays, linked lists, stacks, queues, trees, graphs
- SC1008 C and C++ Programming (3) — SC1003 (NTUMods also accepts SC1303) — C/C++ foundations
- SC2000 Probability & Statistics for Computing (3) — Nil — prob/stats foundation; mutually exclusive MH2500; *Introduction to Databases is SC2207, not SC2000*
- SC2002 Object Oriented Design & Programming (3) — SC1003 or SC1007 — OOP, UML, SOLID

### Year 2 Semester 1
- SC2001 Algorithm Design & Analysis (3) — SC1007 & MH1812 — divide-conquer, greedy, DP, P/NP
- SC2005 Operating Systems (3) — SC1006 & SC1007 — processes, threads, scheduling, memory, FS
- SC2203 Automata, Computability & Complexity (3) — SC2001 co-req (NTUMods also requires SC1007)
- SC2006 Software Engineering (3) — SC2002 co-req
- SC2008 Computer Network (3) — SC2000 & SC1004 (NTUMods allows SC1123/MH2802/AB1202 alt for SC1004)

### Year 2 Semester 2
- SC2207 Introduction to Databases (3) — SC2001 co-req — relational, SQL, normalisation

(Y3/Y4 core: SC3099 Capstone Project 4 AU — Programme Core per PDF — Y3 S1, Year 3 Standing; SC3920 Professional Internship 10 AU — Foundational Core — Y3 S2; SC4079 FYP 8 AU — MPE — Y4. See College Core §2 vs Programme Core §3 bucket note.)

### All "choose A or B" captured
- FYP path: SC4079 (8 AU: 4+4 over Y4, counted as MPE per PDF) OR +3 MPE (9 AU) — MPE 35 AU (FYP path: 27+8) vs 36 AU (non-FYP: 12x3, total 136 unless BDE adjusted per Degree Audit); both enumerated. Highest Distinction requires FYP per PDF Note 3.

## 4. Major Prescribed Electives (MPE) — 35 AU (FYP path) / 36 AU (non-FYP path)

**Rule (U24 and after, verbatim PDF Notes 1–3):**
- Note 1: Complete 9 MPE courses (SC3xxx or SC4xxx), including at least 4 at SC4xxx level.
- Note 2: Students may choose to fulfill this requirement by either completing a Final Year Project (FYP) or by taking 3 additional MPE.
- Note 3: To be eligible for Highest Distinction, students must complete a Final Year Project.
- Hence: FYP path = 9x3=27 AU + SC4079 8 AU =35 AU MPE (total 135 AU). Non-FYP path =12x3=36 AU MPE (total 136 AU unless BDE/overload adjusted — confirm via Degree Audit).
- MPE-1..5: Any SC3xxx/SC4xxx (3 AU each) = 15 AU
- MPE-6..9: Must be SC4xxx (3 AU each) = 12 AU
